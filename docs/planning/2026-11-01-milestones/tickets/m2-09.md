## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** timestamped detections/gaps, **when** processed, **then** accumulate the active seconds defined by D04, excluding paused intervals.
- [ ] **Given** boundary durations and relaunch, **when** displayed, **then** use the approved subminute/rounding rule and restore persisted time.
- [ ] **Given** manual edits or resets, **when** applied, **then** duration follows the approved policy and the edit subtitle is no longer an unsupported promise.

## In scope

Duration accumulator with injected monotonic time, pause/gap semantics, persistence and manual-edit policy.

## Out of scope

Guessing duration from an unapproved steps-per-minute constant.
