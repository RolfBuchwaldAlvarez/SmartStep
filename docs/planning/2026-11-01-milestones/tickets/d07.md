## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** a saved day followed by a height, weight or gender change, **when** the owner decides, **then** examples specify whether past distance/kcal use that day’s profile or the current profile, what happens for same-day changes and later manual step edits, and which profile snapshots or computed metrics the ledger must retain.

- [ ] **Given** an incomplete Monday–Sunday week, **when** accepted, **then** totals, denominator, zero vs absent records and rounding have approved examples for all four metrics.
- [ ] **Given** imperial profile units but report copy says km, **when** accepted, **then** distance labels/conversion and today/no-data clock/minus precedence are explicit.
- [ ] **Given** Back/reopen vs rotation and week navigation, **when** accepted, **then** default/restored tab/week, oldest selectable week and date-label format are specified.

## In scope

Historical-profile policy and required ledger data for M2-01/M2-08/M4-02; incomplete-week average denominator, rounding/imperial units, today-with-no-data icons, historical goal lookup and reopening/recreation state.
