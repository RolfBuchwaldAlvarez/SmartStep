## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** saved profile data, **when** Personal Settings opens, **then** show it under Personal Settings with no Skip or onboarding explanatory subtitle.
- [ ] **Given** fields/pickers, **when** edited and saved, **then** reuse the validated conversions/state logic and persist before returning via the agreed navigation behavior.
- [ ] **Given** returning to Home or later relaunch, **when** navigating, **then** onboarding stays completed and old saved values are not overwritten by initialization.

## In scope

Settings route/mode, owner-agreed save/return behavior, existing field/picker reuse.

## Out of scope

A second profile implementation or automatically repeating onboarding permission prompts.
