## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** activity content, **when** collapsed, **then** show app icon, steps, kcal and progress; expanded adds app name/time and decorative chevron.
- [ ] **Given** content update, **when** notification is replaced, **then** use silent stable update semantics without sound/vibration.
- [ ] **Given** tap or swipe-dismiss, **when** used, **then** tap opens the app and dismissal/reappearance follows the approved platform policy.

## In scope

Channel/content builder, steps/calories/progress, expanded app/time/decorative chevron, tap intent and swipe policy.

## Out of scope

Service lifecycle or a new alert on every update.
