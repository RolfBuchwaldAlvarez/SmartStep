## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** required permissions and approved background eligibility, **when** UI leaves foreground, **then** one app-owned service continues tracking with the required notification.
- [ ] **Given** no background access, **when** UI backgrounds, **then** no service/notification is started and counting remains foreground-only.
- [ ] **Given** app/service recreation or repeated start, **when** running, **then** avoid duplicate listeners/counts and preserve ledger state; API-specific behavior follows D02.

## In scope

Foreground service declaration/start/stop, existing sensor pipeline ownership and notification attachment.

## Out of scope

A second independent counting algorithm or workarounds that bypass Android restrictions.
