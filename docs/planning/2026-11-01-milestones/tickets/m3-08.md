## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** suggestions expanded, **when** shown, **then** exactly Recommend workout, Explain today’s trend, How to reach today’s goal? appear and send their corresponding prompt.
- [ ] **Given** input growth, **when** typing, **then** grow from one to five lines and scroll internally afterward; whitespace-only input cannot send.
- [ ] **Given** wide width, **when** rendered, **then** center suggestions/input/send at fixed 400 dp while preserving content-fit behavior.

## In scope

Collapsible fixed suggestions, single-to-five-line input, internal scrolling and wide 400-dp composer.

## Out of scope

New AI prompts or offline-specific availability decisions.
