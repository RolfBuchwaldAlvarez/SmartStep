## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** active background tracking, **when** Exit is confirmed, **then** app-owned listener/service/notification stop through the D02 mechanism.
- [ ] **Given** subsequent callbacks or restart triggers without an explicit relaunch, **when** received, **then** recording does not restart.
- [ ] **Given** a new user launch, **when** resumed, **then** start a new eligible session without double-counting or losing prior daily data.

## In scope

Wire Exit to listener/service/notification teardown and suppress automatic restarts until explicit relaunch.

## Out of scope

Unsupported force-stop claims or changing the approved product exit policy.
