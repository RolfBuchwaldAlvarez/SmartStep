## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** no background access, **when** drawer opens, **then** repair is first and opens the existing recommendation; granting removes it.
- [ ] **Given** Step Goal, Personal Settings or Exit, **when** selected, **then** the corresponding callback is dispatched once and the drawer closes.
- [ ] **Given** outside tap or swipe-back, **when** used, **then** the existing drawer closes; do not route a user action into an unimplemented TODO.

## In scope

Connect callbacks/navigation destinations; conditional first repair item; close drawer on selection.

## Out of scope
