## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** today-only wording and a selectable date in the PDF, **when** accepted, **then** permitted dates, future-date behavior and historical-edit effects on the current sensor baseline are explicit.
- [ ] **Given** cumulative sensor readings can restart and the date can change, **when** accepted, **then** resets/reboot, day rollover, time-zone changes and first-sample behavior have expected examples with no negative or duplicate counts.
- [ ] **Given** pause then resume, **when** accepted, **then** steps during the pause are excluded and manual edits preserve an offset while future sensor deltas add only once.

## In scope

Date range/validity, historical edits vs live baseline, paused-step exclusion, counter reset/reboot, midnight and time-zone boundaries.

## Out of scope

Writing the ledger or choosing unspecified historical behavior silently.
