## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** the drawer action, **when** sheet opens, **then** the saved goal is selected under a fixed indicator and overflowing content can scroll.
- [ ] **Given** a changed draft, **when** Save succeeds, **then** commit/close/update Home; when Cancel/outside/back closes, then discard and preserve the prior goal.
- [ ] **Given** rotation while open, **when** recreated, **then** sheet and unsaved selection survive; scrolling alone does not dismiss or save.

## In scope
