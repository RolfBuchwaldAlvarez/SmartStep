## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** ≥840 dp, **when** report renders, **then** summary/selector/tabs are centered at 394 dp and day list uses two columns within max 600 dp.
- [ ] **Given** two-column order, **when** read, **then** dates remain chronological Monday–Sunday and each item retains its normal status/semantics.
- [ ] **Given** list scroll or a return to narrow width, **when** layout changes, **then** preserve selection and fixed-component behavior.

## In scope

≥840-dp layout with 394-dp summary/selector/tabs and max-600-dp two-column day container.

## Out of scope

New report behavior or a different ordering of dates.
