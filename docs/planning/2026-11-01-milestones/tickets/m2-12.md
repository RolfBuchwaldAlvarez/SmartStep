## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** seven-day data, **when** rendered, **then** today is rightmost, counts are exact and empty days show 0 with empty progress.
- [ ] **Given** progress zero/half/over-goal, **when** drawn, **then** circles begin at 12 o’clock, advance clockwise and cap at full.
- [ ] **Given** current goal changes, **when** Home updates, **then** only current/future goal interpretation changes, preserving historical progress.
