## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** mobile width, **when** messages render, **then** AI fills available padded width and user bubbles fit text up to 75% of screen width.
- [ ] **Given** wide width, **when** rendered, **then** center history in max 600 dp; AI may fill it and user messages cap at 400 dp.
- [ ] **Given** short/long text and large fonts, **when** rendered, **then** wrap without clipping or reversing chronological message order.

## In scope

Chronological history list, left AI/right user bubbles and adaptive container sizing.

## Out of scope

Input/suggestion behavior or storing conversation between visits.
