## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** steps and height, **when** calculated, **then** use height(cm) × 0.415, centimeters-to-meters and km/mi divisors 1000/1609.3 with one-decimal display.
- [ ] **Given** weight and gender, **when** calculated, **then** use weight(kg) × 0.0005 × factor (male 1.0/female 0.9) per step and whole kcal display.
- [ ] **Given** equivalent metric/imperial profile inputs and zero steps, **when** evaluated, **then** physical results agree within specified rounding and zero stays zero.

- [ ] **Given** profile changes and later edits to saved days, **when** distance/kcal are recalculated, **then** use D07’s approved historical-profile policy and the M2-01 ledger contract, including same-day examples.

## In scope

Pure calculations, canonical units and display rounding; consume profile inputs or stored metrics according to D07’s approved historical-profile policy.

## Out of scope
