## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** 840 dp or wider, **when** onboarding/settings renders, **then** measure actual subtitle/field block and Start bounds at centered 394 dp; a 426-dp outer container with 16-dp side padding is not itself a defect.
- [ ] **Given** narrower widths and large font, **when** content does not fit, **then** fields remain usable and the primary action is reachable.
- [ ] **Given** either picker at phone and wide sizes, **when** opened, **then** it retains its specified size/appearance and dimmed centered presentation.

## In scope

Measure the existing max-426 container minus nested padding, preserve compliant code, and fix only proven 394-dp content/button or breakpoint defects.

## Out of scope

Replacing the whole theme or assigning unspecified pixel colors.
