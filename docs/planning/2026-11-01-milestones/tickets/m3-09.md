## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** offline, **when** composer is tapped, **then** it stays visible but disabled/unfocusable, opens no keyboard and shows Online connection required with inline connection icon.
- [ ] **Given** offline, **when** send/suggestions are attempted, **then** send stays muted/disabled and suggestions follow D06 without issuing a request.
- [ ] **Given** connectivity restored, **when** observed, **then** restore placeholder/input automatically and allow send only for valid nonblank content.

## In scope

Input/send/focus/keyboard states, offline placeholder/icon and approved suggestion behavior.

## Out of scope

Treating reconnect as a reason to restore an old discarded conversation.
