## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** an editing draft, **when** date picker opens, **then** it overlays rather than closes the parent dialog.
- [ ] **Given** month/year changes, **when** dates such as leap day or month end are selected, **then** the result remains valid under D03.
- [ ] **Given** Cancel or Save, **when** returning, **then** preserve the prior date or apply the selected date respectively, keeping step text intact.

## In scope

Independent year/month/day wheels, calendar validity and draft Save/Cancel result.

## Out of scope

Persisting steps or changing owner-approved date bounds.
