## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** normal steps, **when** fewer than 10 new steps accrue since the approved batch baseline, **then** metrics do not recompute; at threshold they update.
- [ ] **Given** edit/reset/foreground refresh, **when** updated data is available, **then** metrics refresh according to D04, including paused-event precedence.
- [ ] **Given** metric/imperial settings, **when** displayed, **then** all three informational metrics show their correct value/unit and remain noninteractive.
