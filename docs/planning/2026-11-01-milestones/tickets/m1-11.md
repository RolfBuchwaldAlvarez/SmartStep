## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** no saved goal, **when** Home loads, **then** use the owner-approved initial goal; given saved goal, then load it after relaunch.
- [ ] **Given** a valid goal change, **when** persisted, **then** Home goal/progress update from the observable source.
- [ ] **Given** invalid/out-of-range input or save failure, **when** rejected, **then** the previous goal remains; fake step data can exercise progress without claiming real tracking exists.

## In scope

Small goal repository/use cases and observable Home state; saved initialization and range validation.

## Out of scope

Sensor tracking or historical per-day goals, which arrive in milestone 2.
