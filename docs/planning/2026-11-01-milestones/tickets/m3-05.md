## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Given** a new online Coach entry, **when** initialized, **then** start with empty history and request one concise greeting/introduction/context/question starter automatically.
- [ ] **Given** offline entry and later connectivity, **when** handled, **then** follow the D06 starter policy without duplicate initial messages.
- [ ] **Given** Back/disposal and another entry, **when** started, **then** discard the prior conversation and build a new current-context starter.

## In scope

Session-scoped conversation state, initial predefined prompt and disposal contract.

## Out of scope

Persisting chat or reusing Home insight conversation state.
